Get the extension

Add Unmasked to Chrome

The download is the unpacked extension. Load that folder, then use Ask on the product page.

  1. 1

    Download the extension and unzip it.

  2. 2

    Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

  3. 3

    Choose Load unpacked and select the unmasked folder.

  4. 4

    Open a product page and sign in with the same email and password as this site. Orders, the wishlist, and recommendations stay on that account.
