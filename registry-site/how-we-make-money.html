How affiliate commission works

The price you pay does not change. Retailers run affiliate programs and pay a commission, usually a small percentage of the sale, out of their advertising budget, because they only pay when a sale happens.

  • We would only attach a link when you click through from one of our verdicts.
  • We never overwrite another creator’s or site’s link at checkout.
  • We will disclose which retailers we earn from, and follow each program’s rules for browser extensions.

The firewall between money and verdicts

  • Verdict first. The checking system never sees commission data. Links are added only after a verdict is final.
  • Pay only on passes. Flagged products get no link, so a bad finding never earns us money.
  • No pay for placement. Nobody can pay for a better score, a softer verdict or a removed flag.
  • Ranking by trust and cost. Commission rates never decide the order.
  • Open methodology. We publish how verdicts are made, including their limits.

What we will not do

  • Sell your browsing data.
  • Let a brand pay to remove or soften a finding.
  • Hide affiliate links or add them silently.
  • Present a pass as an endorsement. It means the claims held up under our checks.

Conflicts we admit to

Earning commission only on passing products gives us a reason to pass more of them. To keep ourselves honest, we publish our methodology and the agent activity behind each score, show findings companies dispute, and require any brand badge to come with published failures too. You can check all of it in the public registry.

Our pricing commitments (draft)

  • Verdicts and scores are never for sale. No plan changes what our agents find.
  • The core checks stay free.
  • We announce prices, and give notice, before charging anyone.
  • We do not sell your browsing data.

This describes our plan. Nothing here is charged or earned yet. Prices are not set and plans may change. Last updated October 17, 2026.
